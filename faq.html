Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, care, and our mission.

What does buy one, give one mean?

For every veil purchased, one veil is gifted to a sister in need.

What materials are used?

Our veils use soft lace and lightweight fabrics selected for comfort and graceful drape.

How do I choose a veil?

Start with style and color you love. Each product page includes short notes for feel and finish.

How do I care for my veil?

Hand wash cold, lay flat to dry, and store folded in a soft pouch.

Can I return or exchange my order?

Return and exchange policy text will be finalized in the next pass.

Who receives the gifted veils?

Gifted veils are shared with sisters in need through community-informed outreach.
